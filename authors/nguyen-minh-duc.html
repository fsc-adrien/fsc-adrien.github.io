About Nguyen Minh Duc

Nguyen Minh Duc is a Senior Technical Consultant and Software Architect at FSC Software, where he leads the technical strategy practice and authors FSC's engineering blog. With over 12 years of enterprise software experience, Duc specializes in guiding organizations through high-stakes technology transformations — from cloud migrations and microservices re-architectures to AI/ML implementations and DevOps cultural shifts.

Duc has served as lead architect on engagements across financial services, healthcare, e-commerce, and SaaS sectors, consistently delivering measurable outcomes: reduced deployment cycles, lower infrastructure costs, and accelerated feature delivery. His hands-on consulting style bridges the gap between technical execution and business strategy, ensuring that technology investments generate quantifiable ROI.

Areas of Expertise

  • Cloud Architecture & Migration: AWS, Azure, GCP — including serverless, containerized, and hybrid architectures. Led 15+ zero-downtime cloud migrations.
  • Microservices & Distributed Systems: Domain-driven design, event-driven architecture, service mesh (Istio, Linkerd), API gateway patterns.
  • DevOps Transformation: CI/CD pipeline design (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins), infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Ansible), SRE practices, observability (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK).
  • AI/ML Strategy & Implementation: ML pipeline architecture, MLOps (MLflow, Kubeflow), LLM integration, recommendation systems, and data platform design (Snowflake, BigQuery, dbt).
  • Security & Compliance: HIPAA, SOC2, GDPR, PCI-DSS compliance frameworks. Zero-trust architecture design and security automation.
