Why Enterprise Retail Needs a Specialized Development Partner

Enterprise retail software fails in predictable ways: platforms collapse under Black Friday traffic because load testing was inadequate, inventory discrepancies cause unfulfillable orders that erode customer trust, and monolithic architectures prevent the rapid channel expansion that modern omnichannel retail demands. FSC Software's retail practice has delivered platforms generating $500M+ in combined GMV annually, handling 100K+ daily orders across multiple channels. We understand that in retail, system downtime during peak events is not a technical problem — it's a revenue and brand problem.

Headless Commerce Architecture

Headless commerce decouples the frontend presentation layer from the backend commerce engine, enabling brands to deliver fast, personalized experiences across web, mobile, and emerging channels without platform constraints. FSC Software architects headless stacks using Next.js or Nuxt.js frontends connected to Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, or custom commerce backends via GraphQL APIs. Performance results: Lighthouse scores of 95+, LCP under 2.0 seconds, and Core Web Vitals passing on mobile — directly correlated to conversion rate improvements of 15–25%.

We implement Incremental Static Regeneration (ISR) for product catalog pages (sub-50ms TTFB for cached pages), edge-deployed personalization via Vercel Edge Functions or Cloudflare Workers, and Progressive Web App (PWA) capabilities for offline browsing and push notifications. For brands with extensive product catalogs (100K+ SKUs), we implement search-as-a-service (Algolia, Elastic App Search) delivering faceted search results in under 50ms.

Shopify Plus Custom Development

FSC Software is an experienced Shopify Plus development partner. We build custom Shopify themes using Liquid + React (Hydrogen), develop private Shopify apps for business-specific functionality, implement Shopify Flow automations for order routing and fulfillment logic, and customize Shopify Plus checkout using Checkout UI Extensions and Functions. We also implement Shopify B2B features for wholesale operations: company accounts, custom pricing, net payment terms, and quote management.

Our Shopify Plus implementations have handled peak traffic of 50,000+ concurrent sessions during promotional events with zero downtime, using pre-sale Shopify optimizations: variant preloading, cart drawer optimization, and async checkout initiation to reduce queue abandonment.

Omnichannel Inventory & Order Management

We build centralized Order Management Systems (OMS) that serve as a single source of truth across all channels and fulfillment locations. Core capabilities: real-time inventory sync across Shopify, Amazon, eBay, and brick-and-mortar POS systems; distributed order routing logic (route to nearest warehouse, split shipments, handle backorders automatically); buy-online-pickup-in-store (BOPIS) and ship-from-store workflows; returns management automation reducing manual processing by 65%.

We integrate with warehouse management systems (WMS) including Manhattan Associates, HighJump, and Fishbowl, and with 3PL providers (ShipBob, Deliverr, Ryder) via EDI and API. Inventory accuracy targets we achieve: 99.7%+ across all channels, stock-out rate reduction of 60–70% through demand forecasting.

Multi-Channel Expansion

We handle the technical complexity of expanding to new sales channels: Amazon Seller Central and Vendor Central integrations (listing sync, FBA inventory management, Buy Box optimization), eBay marketplace integration, Google Shopping and Performance Max feed management, TikTok Shop integration for social commerce, Instagram/Facebook Shopping, and Pinterest Buyable Pins. Each channel has distinct data formats, inventory rules, and order management APIs — we abstract this complexity into a single operational interface for your team.

Multi-channel clients typically see 40–50% revenue increase within 12 months of expansion, with 35–45% of new revenue coming from channels that didn't exist before engagement.

AI-Powered Personalization

Personalization at scale requires ML infrastructure, not just rules-based merchandising. FSC Software builds recommendation engines using collaborative filtering (user-item matrix factorization), content-based filtering (product attribute similarity), and hybrid approaches. We implement real-time personalization for: homepage product carousels (updating based on session behavior), "frequently bought together" and "customers also viewed" modules, personalized email product recommendations via Klaviyo or Attentive integrations, and dynamic landing pages for paid search that match ad creative to product display.

Typical personalization lift: 10–22% AOV increase, 8–15% conversion rate improvement on personalized vs. control pages in A/B tests.

PCI-DSS Compliance & Payment Integration

PCI-DSS compliance for retail encompasses both the payment platform and all systems that touch cardholder data. We implement: Stripe, Adyen, Braintree, and regional payment provider integrations with tokenization to eliminate PAN storage from your systems; 3D Secure 2.0 authentication to reduce friction while meeting SCA requirements; recurring billing and subscription management for DTC subscription boxes; and buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) integrations (Afterpay, Klarna, Affirm). Network tokenization further reduces false declines by 10–15% compared to traditional tokens.
