Overview of E-commerce Platform Development

FSC Software specializes in end-to-end e-commerce solutions that drive revenue growth and customer engagement. From Shopify Plus custom development to BigCommerce implementations and fully custom platforms, we deliver scalable, secure, and high-performing digital retail ecosystems. With 50+ successful deployments generating $500M+ in GMV annually for clients, we understand the unique challenges of building platforms that scale globally.

E-commerce Platform Selection & Architecture

Strategic platform selection based on business model, scale requirements, and budget. Shopify Plus for enterprise retailers handling 1000+ orders/day with custom storefronts. BigCommerce for B2B and B2C enterprises requiring open architecture. Custom platforms on Node.js or Python for unique business requirements. Marketplace development for multi-vendor ecosystems. Each platform choice optimized for specific use cases: Shopify excels in fashion/retail, BigCommerce in wholesale, custom platforms in specialized domains.

Payment Integration & Compliance

Seamless integration with Stripe, PayPal, Square, Razorpay, and regional payment providers. PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance ensures security for sensitive data. Multi-currency support for global merchants. Fraud prevention and chargeback protection. Escrow and holding account management for marketplaces. Real-time payment reconciliation and reporting dashboards. Average transaction security: 99.99% uptime, <100ms latency for payment processing.

Real-Time Inventory Management

Sync inventory across multiple sales channels: website, Amazon, eBay, Shopify, Instagram. Real-time stock updates prevent overselling. Backorder management with automated fulfillment. SKU-level tracking for product variants. Integration with warehouse management systems (WMS). Demand forecasting using historical data and trends. Typical inventory accuracy: 99.8%, stock-out reduction: 60-70%.

Multi-Channel Selling & Commerce

Centralized inventory and unified order management across channels. Channel-specific optimization for Amazon Advertising, eBay Seller Central, Google Shopping. Social commerce: Instagram Shopping, TikTok Shop, Pinterest integration. Buy-online-pickup-in-store (BOPIS) functionality. Conversion tracking across channels. Typical revenue increase: 40-50% from multi-channel expansion, average order value increase: 15-25%.

Personalized product recommendations increasing AOV by 10-20%. Advanced analytics dashboards tracking KPIs: conversion rate, average order value, customer lifetime value, ROAS. Monthly optimization recommendations based on data analysis.

Page load time under 2.5 seconds (Core Web Vitals). Mobile-first responsive design. Progressive Web App (PWA) capabilities for offline browsing. Performance: 99.95% uptime SLA, <100ms response times, auto-scaling for traffic spikes.
